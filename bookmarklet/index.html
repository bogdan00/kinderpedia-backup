Common Questions

Quick answers if you get stuck or want to run incremental updates later.

Are my child's photos private?
Yes — 100%. The bookmarklet runs entirely inside your own logged-in Kinderpedia browser tab and saves photos, videos, and documents straight from Kinderpedia to your computer's hard drive. Neither your password nor your family photos are ever uploaded to any third-party storage server.
Why do I need Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Brave?
Saving tens of gigabytes of full-resolution photos and videos directly into organized folders without running out of memory uses a modern browser feature (File System Access) currently supported on desktop Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Opera. Safari, Firefox, and phone browsers cannot write folders directly to disk.
Can I run it again next month to grab only new albums?
Yes! Simply open Kinderpedia, click the bookmark, and pick your existing backup folder. Any already-downloaded albums and videos are skipped automatically. (If you want to pull newly posted albums after a completed backup, delete albums_index.json and videos_index.json inside Metadata/<Child>/, or use the Python CLI's --refresh-index flag.)
